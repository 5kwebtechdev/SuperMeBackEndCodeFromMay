Delete Your SuperMe Account

We’re sorry to see you go. Here’s how you can permanently delete your account and data.

🗑️ Request account deletion

You can request deletion in two ways. Both methods require you to confirm your identity.

📱

From within the SuperMe app

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Follow the on-screen instructions.

✉️

Contact support

Send an email from your registered email address to support@supermeapp.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Include your username and registered phone/email.

⏱️ Deletion timeline: After we receive a verified request, your account and all associated data (moods, tasks, habits, coins, badges, family links) will be permanently deleted within 7 days.
⚠️ Important: Deletion is irreversible. You cannot recover any data once the process completes.
Need help or have questions?
Reach us at support@supermeapp.com
We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
